THE TOTALVAC STANDARD

Service that respects the site.

Vacuum work often happens where downtime, access, cleanliness, and communication matter. TotalVac is built to make the process straightforward from the first call to the final check.

  • Prepared for the job

    We confirm the material, access, hose reach, site conditions, and scheduling needs before service whenever possible.

  • Clear communication

    Customers know what is being scheduled, what information is needed, and when the work is complete.

  • Clean work practices

    The service approach is planned to protect surrounding areas and leave the work zone orderly.

  • Flexible service

    Book a one-time pump-out or discuss a recurring maintenance schedule based on the needs of the property.

HOW IT WORKS

Simple from request to removal.

  1. Tell us what needs service

    Share the location, material type, access details, approximate volume, urgency, and any photos that help us assess the job.

  2. Confirm the scope

    We review the service requirements, confirm whether the material is accepted, and coordinate a suitable service window.

  3. Complete the pump-out

    The crew arrives prepared, performs the approved work, checks the service area, and confirms completion.

  4. Plan the next service

    For recurring needs, we can discuss a maintenance interval based on usage and site conditions.
