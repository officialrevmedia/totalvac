Vacuum services across Kitchener, Waterloo and the Greater Toronto Area.

TotalVac serves approved commercial, industrial, food-service, property, and worksite locations across Kitchener, Waterloo and the Greater Toronto Area. Service availability depends on location, material type, access, volume, scheduling, and disposal requirements.
