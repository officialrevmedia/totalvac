## Verification: what to prepare

Video verification is now the default for new listings. It is one continuous recording, no cuts and no edits, and most first attempts fail for avoidable reasons.

Record this sequence in a single take:

1. Start at the street showing the address number at 15 David Bergey Drive
2. Walk toward the trailer without stopping the recording
3. Show the trailer clearly, including any TotalVac branding and the plate
4. Open a compartment and show hoses, fittings and equipment
5. Finish by showing you run the business: unlock the vehicle, open the invoicing or banking app on the phone, or show business cards and printed material

Three things that make the difference:

- **Get the decal on the trailer first.** Branding on the equipment is one of the strongest signals in a video verification, and the cards and website will match it.
- **Do not stop recording.** A cut is treated as a red flag.
- **Have the website live first.** Google cross-checks the business name, phone and service area against the website during verification. The site must be at totalvacsolutions.com with the phone number visible, ideally live for a week beforehand.

If verification is refused twice, request a different method through support rather than resubmitting the same footage.

## After verification

**Photos.** Upload the logo (use `profile-dark-1080x1080.png` from the brand pack) and the cover (`link-preview-1200x630.png`). Then add real photographs of the trailer and real jobs. Do not upload the generated images that are currently on the website.

**Reviews.** This is what actually moves you in the map pack. After each of the first jobs, send the review link from the dashboard and ask the customer to mention the city and the service in their own words. Ten honest reviews naming "grease trap" and "Kitchener" will outperform any amount of on-page tuning. Reply to every one.

**Posts.** Weekly posts lift click-through in the local panel. The three post templates in the brand pack are sized for this.

**Consistency.** Name, phone and address must be identical on the website, the profile, the business cards and any directory listing. Inconsistency is the quiet reason many local listings underperform.

## What will get the profile suspended

Keywords in the business name. A PO box, mailbox rental or virtual office. More than one listing for the same business. Service areas the business does not genuinely cover. Changing the address or the primary category repeatedly right after verifying.
